Colour

Contrast is measured against --bg-void. WCAG AA wants 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text; anything below 3:1 should only ever be decoration. Surfaces show no ratio (they are the background), and translucent tokens show their alpha instead — a colour you can see through has no fixed contrast.
